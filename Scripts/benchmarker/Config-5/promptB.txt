## TACTICAL RULES & DECISION FLOW

**A. ATTACK PHASE (Your First Priority)**
- **Can you attack a VALID target RIGHT NOW?**
    - **VALID TARGETS:** `Enemy Ship` or `Neutral Target (🎯)`. Attacking `Ally Ship` is an illegal, game-losing mistake; attacking `Empty Tile` wastes your attack.
    - **RANGE:** Target must be at Manhattan distance exactly `2` from you (count orthogonal steps only; a diagonal step counts as 2). Tiles at distance `1` or closer are a dead zone and cannot be attacked.
    *Example:* If you are at position (10,9) and your offset is 1 and range is 2, then you can attack positions: (10,11),(11,10),(9,10),(12,9),(8,9),(9,8),(11,8),(10,7).
- **Wave-Propagation Tactic:** Attacking a `Wave` tile (`~^`, `~V`, `~<`, `~>`) damages the wave tile and then propagates the attack **one tile in the direction the wave arrow points**. The propagated hit damages whatever is on that next tile — enemy, ally, neutral, or obstacle. The wave does **not** let you choose an arbitrary target beyond it; the direction is fixed by the arrow. So a wave attack only reaches an enemy if that enemy is exactly one tile beyond the wave in its arrow direction, and you can hit the wave tile itself. **Warning:** if an ally is one tile beyond the wave in its arrow direction, the propagation will damage your ally. Attacking a wave with no enemy beyond it usually wastes your attack.

**B. MOVEMENT PHASE (Only if attacking now is impossible or ineffective)**
- **Purpose:** Move **only** to achieve one of these goals:
    1.  **Close Distance:** To get a priority enemy into your attack range.
    2.  **Line Up Shot:** To position yourself to use a wave for an extended attack next turn.
    3.  **Evade Danger:** To move away from clusters of enemies or dangerous wave paths.
- **Path Validation:** Movement is tile-by-tile. Your path **MUST NOT** cross a Wave tile (causes early stop) or a blocked tile (Ship), even if your final destination is otherwise reachable. Use the provided safe/risky position lists.
